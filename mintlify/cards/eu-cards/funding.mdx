An EU card spends straight from the balance of the account it's linked
to. There's no separate card balance to top up. To fund the card, add
money to that account.

## Which account a card spends from

Each EU card is linked to exactly one of the cardholder's own accounts:

- their default EUR account, or
- their USDC account, or
- their BTC account.

The card always has `currency: EUR`. When it's linked to the USDC or BTC
account, the card issuer converts each purchase from EUR at the time of
the purchase.

To link the card to a different account later, send its id as
`fundingSource` in `PATCH /cards/{id}`. The same rules apply as at
issuance.

## Add money to the account

Each of the cardholder's accounts lists its deposit details in
`fundingPaymentInstructions`. See
[Internal accounts](/payouts-and-b2b/depositing-funds/internal-accounts)
for how to read them.

- **EUR account:** an IBAN. The cardholder can send euros to it by SEPA
  transfer.
- **USDC or BTC account:** a deposit address on each network the account
  supports.

## Open banking

If Lightspark has enabled open banking for your platform, you can also
start a payment from the cardholder's own bank into their EUR account.
Call `POST /internal-accounts/{id}/open-banking-payments` and send the
cardholder to the returned `authorizationUrl`. They approve the payment in
their banking app.

See [Open banking](/payouts-and-b2b/depositing-funds/open-banking) for the
full flow.
