Your physical card and its wallet art share one design: the front of the
card. You design the front and choose a few options for the back. We supply
the chip, the magnetic stripe, the Visa marks, the issuer statement, and the
production files, and we take the design through Visa's review.

<Note>
Physical cards are set up with your Lightspark contact as part of card
program onboarding. The Cards API issues virtual cards; see
[Issuing cards](/cards/card-management/issuing-cards).
</Note>

## What you design and what we supply

| Surface | You design | We supply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front | Background color or artwork, your logo and where it sits | Chip module |
| Back | Background color (usually the front's), personalization ink color and typeface, your support phone number and URL | Magnetic stripe, account block layout, contactless indicator, Visa DEBIT lockup in silver foil, "Issued by Lead Bank" statement |
| Wallet art | Nothing extra: we derive it from the front | Visa DEBIT lockup, PNG export, submission to Visa and the wallets |

## Two production bodies

Cards are printed on one of two bodies. Pick the one that fits your brand.

<CardSamples />

- **Monochrome**: black-core plastic. Personalization prints in white. Your logo prints in white or in one spot color (give us a Pantone number or a hex value). Finishes: matte, gloss, or satin. The card edge is black.
- **Full color**: CMYK print on white-core plastic. Any color, gradient, or artwork, printed edge to edge on both faces. On the back, the stripe band bleeds to the top edge. The card edge is white.

Both bodies carry the Visa DEBIT lockup as silver hot-stamped foil on the back.

## Canvas

Work on a 1536 × 969 px artboard. It has the proportions of an ISO/IEC 7810
ID-1 card, and it is also the size Visa requires for wallet art, so one file
serves both.

| | Physical | On the artboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Width | 85.6 mm | 1536 px |
| Height | 53.98 mm | 969 px |
| Corner radius | 3.175 mm | 57 px |
| Thickness | 0.76 mm | |
| Scale | 1 mm | 17.94 px |
| Type | 1 pt | 6.33 px |

Keep the artboard's corners square and run backgrounds to the edge. The
manufacturer cuts the corners; preview them at 57 px if you want to see the
result. Anything that must stay legible (logos, text) stays inside a 54 px
(3 mm) safe margin.

## Front

The front carries your brand and the chip. Nothing else.

- **Chip**: the module sits at (152, 324) and measures 236 × 164 px. It is embedded after printing, so nothing prints over it. Keep the zone from x 107 to 415 and y 279 to 533 clear of logos and text; background color and artwork may run through it.
- **Logo**: the samples put it right of the chip, centered on the chip's mid-line (y 406), with its right edge 152 px from the card edge, 90 px tall. A larger mark, a centered mark, or a bleeding watermark all work if they stay out of the chip zone and legible parts stay inside the safe margin.
- **Artwork**: photographs, gradients, and patterns are fine on the full-color body. Supply them at the artboard size or larger; for raster art, 300 dpi at card size is 1011 × 638 px, so a 1536 × 969 file already exceeds it.

Do not put on the front:

- Cardholder name, card number, expiry date, or security code. These print on the back.
- A drawn chip, hologram, or any imitation of a security feature.
- Visa marks. We place the Visa DEBIT lockup on the back from Visa's supplied artwork.
- Taglines, unless they are part of your trademarked logo.
- URLs or phone numbers. The back carries your support line.

## Back

The back is a fixed layout. Your background color or artwork continues onto
it, and you choose how the personalization prints.

<CardBlueprint face="back" />

| Element | Position | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magnetic stripe | y 72 to 300, full width | Standard black. On the full-color body the band extends to the top edge. |
| Account block | Left edge 56 px, from y 476 | Cardholder name, card number, expiry, and security code, each on its own line in 57 px (9 pt) type. Printed at personalization. |
| Contactless indicator | Right edge 1482, y 470, 90 px tall | EMVCo artwork, placed by us. |
| Visa DEBIT lockup | 339 × 211 px at (1143, 704) | Silver foil. 54 px clear space on every side, kept free of text and logos. |
| Support line | Left edge 56 px, y 876, 22 px type | Your support phone number and URL. |
| Issuer statement | Left edge 56 px, y 902, 22 px type | "Issued by Lead Bank". Fixed. |

Your choices on the back:

- **Background**: the front's color or artwork, or a solid color from your palette. Keep the area under the lockup calm enough for silver foil to read.
- **Personalization ink**: white or dark, whichever contrasts with the background. The monochrome body prints white only.
- **Typeface**: a sans serif. The default is Suisse Intl Regular at 57 px (9 pt), the minimum Visa allows for a printed cardholder name; up to 12 pt is permitted.
- **Support line**: the phone number and URL your cardholders should use.

## Color

- Everything printed must contrast with what is behind it. This is a Visa requirement for the cardholder name and a legibility requirement for everything else.
- Give us brand colors as hex values, plus Pantone or CMYK values if your brand guide has them. Full-color cards print in CMYK, so screen colors shift slightly; Pantone references help us match.
- The monochrome body allows one spot color for the logo. Give its Pantone number.
- The Visa DEBIT lockup is always silver foil. You do not choose its color.

## Wallet art

When a cardholder adds the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay, the wallet shows
a flat image of the card. We produce it from your front design.

<CardBlueprint face="wallet" />

The image Visa accepts is a 1536 × 969 px PNG at 72 dpi, RGB, with square
corners (the wallet rounds them). Visa's rules for what may appear on it:

- No cardholder name, card number, expiry date, chip, hologram, or embossing cues.
- No 3D or skeuomorphic effects that imitate a physical card.
- The lower-left quadrant stays empty. The wallet draws the last four digits there.
- The Visa DEBIT lockup sits in the lower right. We add it.

If your logo sits right of the chip on the physical card, it lands in the
upper right of the wallet art, which is allowed. A logo that depends on the
chip for balance, or one placed in the lower left, needs an alternate
placement for the wallet. Tell us if you want to supply one.

## What to send

Send the following to your Lightspark contact:

- Your logo as a vector file (SVG, PDF, or EPS), in the color it prints in.
- Brand colors as hex values, plus Pantone or CMYK if you have them.
- The front design on a 1536 × 969 artboard, as the layered source file (Figma, Illustrator, or Sketch) or a PDF with vectors preserved. Include the overlay template on a hidden layer.
- Your choices for the back: background color, personalization ink, typeface, support phone number and URL.
- The body: monochrome or full color, and the finish for monochrome.

## Outside these guidelines

For a design that does not fit these guidelines, such as a different
orientation, material, or back layout, contact your Lightspark contact
before you start.
