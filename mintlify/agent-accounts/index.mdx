Let your customers connect an AI agent to their account and hand it real financial work: getting paid, paying people and suppliers, buying online, and splitting revenue. This works for individuals and for businesses. A business can give each team member's agent its own budget on one shared balance, pay contractors who have no account, and pay a whole list of people in one approved run.

The customer keeps ownership of the account. Grid checks every agent action against the policy the customer set, and either runs it, refuses it, or holds it for the customer to approve. The agent can run in an AI app, on the customer's device, or in your own product. Grid does not run the agent. It provides the agent's credentials, policy checks, approvals and the audit trail.

## For businesses

A business customer, verified through KYB, can run its money through agents the same way an individual can, with controls built for a team:

- **Bank details to get paid.** Once verified, a business gets a US bank account and routing number: the `USD_ACCOUNT` entry in the `fundingPaymentInstructions` of its USD internal account. Clients pay it by ordinary bank transfer.
- **Invoicing.** The [Lightspark connector](/agent-accounts/ai-app-connector) lets an agent send a client an invoice with its own payment page and matches the payment to the invoice. The Agents API has no invoice object; if you invoice from your own product, point payers at the bank details above.
- **An agent per person.** Each agent records which team member it acts for (`actingFor`), and that reference is copied onto every card it is issued.
- **Team budgets on one balance.** Put agents in a [spending pool](/agent-accounts/spending-limits) to cap what they spend together each week, each month or in total, and which kinds of payment they may make. Pools hold no money, so there is nothing to move between them.
- **Pay contractors and suppliers.** Pay someone by phone number or email with a [claimable payout](/agent-accounts/payments#pay-a-person-by-phone-or-email), one at a time or in a [payout run](/agent-accounts/payments#pay-many-people-at-once).
- **Share revenue.** [Settlement rules](/agent-accounts/payments#split-incoming-payments) split incoming payments by the shares the business approves.

## Key concepts

| Concept | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | The person or business whose money the agent spends. An agent belongs to exactly one customer and can only see and act on that customer's accounts. |
| **Agent** | A delegated actor with a name, a policy, and its own credential (`Agent:...`). Your backend creates it; the agent software connects by redeeming a one-time device code. |
| **Policy** | What the agent may do (permissions), how it executes (automatically or only with approval), how much it may spend (spending limits), which accounts it may use, and above what amount it must ask. |
| **Agent action** | Every money movement an agent asks for becomes an `AgentAction` (`AgentAction:...`). Grid executes it at once or holds it in `PENDING_APPROVAL` until your platform approves or rejects it. |
| **Spending pool** | A limit several of a customer's agents share, on top of each agent's own policy. A pool holds no money. |
| **Delegated key** | A Grid-custodied signing key the customer authorizes once, so Grid can sign the agent's approved sends from their Global Account without the customer signing each one. |
| **Cards** | Agents can be issued their own virtual cards, request single-use or recurring purchase cards, or use a card the customer already owns that you stored for them. |

## How it fits with Global Accounts

An agent's sends and purchase cards are paid from the customer's [Global Account](/global-accounts), the self-custodial Embedded Wallet (`type: EMBEDDED_WALLET`) Grid provisions for them. Outbound payments from a Global Account normally need the customer's signature on every send. For an agent, the customer instead authorizes a delegated key for that agent and wallet once, and Grid signs only the sends its policy and the customer's approvals allow. The customer can pause the agent, revoke the key, or delete the agent at any time.
