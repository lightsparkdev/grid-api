An agent spends from the customer's Global Account, so funding an agent account means funding that Embedded Wallet. Incoming funds never need the customer's signature or approval. There are two ways in: your platform funds the account as it funds any internal account, or the agent creates a deposit target the customer (or anyone) pays.

## Funded by your platform

A Global Account is an internal account, so every way you already fund internal accounts works. Create a quote with the Global Account's `InternalAccount` id as the `destination`, from any source currency your platform supports, and Grid converts into the wallet's currency on execute. A real-time funding source returns `paymentInstructions` the customer follows to pay in.

See [Fund the account](/global-accounts/implementation-overview#4-fund-the-account) in the Global Accounts walkthrough for the request.

### Bank transfers to the customer's account details

Once a business customer is verified, its USD internal account carries a US account and routing number: the `USD_ACCOUNT` entry in its `fundingPaymentInstructions`. The account number is unique to that account, so its clients can pay it by ordinary bank transfer without a reference.

## Deposits the agent creates

With the `RECEIVE_FUNDS` permission, the agent can create a deposit target for the customer's account with `POST /agents/me/deposits`. `Idempotency-Key` is required: a retry with the same key returns the same deposit.

| `sourceNetwork` | `sourceCurrency` | What the agent gets |
|---|---|---|
| `LIGHTNING` | `USD` | A BOLT11 invoice for an exact USD `amount` (in cents, required), with an expiry |
| `BASE`, `ETHEREUM`, `SOLANA`, `POLYGON`, `ARBITRUM` | The asset sent on that chain | A deposit address on that chain. No amount is needed |
| `TRON` | `USDT` only | A deposit address on Tron |

`destinationInternalAccountId` must belong to the agent's customer and be allowed by the agent's policy.

To pull from the customer's own bank account instead, see [Pull from the customer's bank account](#pull-from-the-customers-bank-account).

<CodeGroup>
```bash Lightning
curl -X POST "$GRID_BASE_URL/agents/me/deposits" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AGENT_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: 2c7e9a1f-5b3d-4e8a-9c0f-6d1b4e8a2c75" \
  -d '{
    "sourceNetwork": "LIGHTNING",
    "sourceCurrency": "USD",
    "destinationInternalAccountId": "InternalAccount:019542f5-b3e7-1d02-0000-000000000002",
    "amount": 2000
  }'
```


### Lightning deposits

The customer pays exactly `amount` in USD; deposit fees are deducted before the destination is credited, and `estimatedReceiveAmount` shows the estimate. The invoice must be paid by `expiresAt`. `paymentLinks.cashApp` opens the invoice in Cash App, and `paymentLinks.shortUrl` is a shortened link when one is available.

### Chain deposits

A chain request returns a standing address that does not expire. Grid counts the first payment the address receives from that network in that currency within 24 hours of the request. While that window is open with nothing paid, a second request for the same account, network and currency is refused with `409`; the error gives the time the window closes.

Where Grid funds a chain deposit through a quote instead, the address is the quote's, the deposit carries the quote's `expiresAt`, and `quoteId` names the quote. A payment that arrives after it expired is not converted: the deposit stays `EXPIRED`, `paidAfterExpiry` becomes `true`, and Lightspark recovers the funds by hand. Do not send it again.

### Tracking a deposit

Read a deposit with `GET /agents/me/deposits/{depositId}`. `status` is `PENDING`, `PROCESSING`, `COMPLETED`, `EXPIRED`, `FAILED` or `REFUNDED`. The transaction that pays a deposit carries the deposit's `quoteId`, so you can match them, and your platform receives the usual `INCOMING_PAYMENT` webhook. Except for bank pulls, a deposit reported `FAILED` or `EXPIRED` can still become `COMPLETED` when funds arrive late; you then receive `INCOMING_PAYMENT` for its transaction again, now `COMPLETED`.

A `503` means the deposit provider is unavailable or holding the order for review. Retry with the same `Idempotency-Key`.

### Pull from the customer's bank account

Where your platform enables external-account pulls, the agent can ask to pull USD from one of the customer's own US bank accounts into their USDB Global Account with `sourceNetwork: ACH_PULL`. Until then, the request is refused with `403` (`ACH_PULL_NOT_ENABLED`).

1. Your backend validates the customer's bank account through Plaid and records it with `POST /agents/{agentId}/external-accounts/{externalAccountId}/ach-validations`. A pull needs an `ACTIVE` USD external account of the agent's customer with a recorded validation.
2. The agent creates the deposit with `POST /agents/me/deposits`, giving `sourceCurrency: USD`, `amount` in cents, `sourceExternalAccountId`, and the customer's USDB `EMBEDDED_WALLET` as `destinationInternalAccountId`. This moves no money. The deposit is `PENDING` and carries `achPull.authorization.text`, the debit authorization the customer must accept, and `achPull.limits`, how much more the customer can pull.
3. Your product shows that text verbatim to the signed-in customer (for a business, an admin). When they accept, your backend calls `POST /agents/{agentId}/deposits/{depositId}/authorize-debit` with platform credentials, sending the text's `textVersion` and `textSha256`.

On success the debit is submitted, the deposit becomes `PROCESSING`, and the amount is credited to the Global Account at once. It becomes `COMPLETED` when the debit settles, or `FAILED` if Grid refuses it or the bank returns it (`achPull.returnCode`). A deposit that is not authorized within 30 minutes becomes `EXPIRED`. The agent can never authorize a debit itself.

The customer will authorize the pull from their own debit card. Grid stores the card encrypted, as it does for [stored cards](/agent-accounts/cards#stored-cards), so your platform never handles the card number.

## Limits before verification

Until a business customer's KYB status is `APPROVED`, its agents' sends and deposits are held to the same limits as an unverified individual's, counted across the whole business. With `MANAGE_IDENTITY`, an agent can help an individual customer finish verification: read and update identity details (`GET`/`PATCH /agents/me/customer`), submit for KYC (`POST /agents/me/verifications`), or generate a single-use hosted KYC link (`POST /agents/me/kyc-link`).

## Funding in sandbox

In sandbox, credit any internal account instantly with `POST /sandbox/internal-accounts/{accountId}/fund`. See [Sandbox testing](/agent-accounts/platform-tools/sandbox-testing).
