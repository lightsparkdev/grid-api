A manual cancellation happens when your customer asks for a payout to be
cancelled, and the sender is refunded. Use these suffixes on the external
account a quote pays out to. They work the same way as the
[quote destination test patterns](/api-reference/sandbox-testing#creating-quotes).

| Suffix | Behavior |
|--------|----------|
| **006** | Cancelled after completion — the payment completes, then about two minutes later the sender is refunded |
| **008** | Cancelled while processing — the sender is refunded about a minute after execution, then the payment completes about two minutes later |

In both cases the refund has `reason: USER_CANCELLATION`, and the transaction
status stays `COMPLETED`. Check the transaction's `refund` object to see that
the sender got the money back.

## Webhook order

**006 — cancelled after completion:**

1. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.PENDING`
2. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.PROCESSING`
3. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.COMPLETED`
4. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.REFUND_PENDING`
5. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.REFUND_COMPLETED`

**008 — cancelled while processing:**

1. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.PENDING`
2. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.PROCESSING`
3. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.REFUND_PENDING`
4. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.REFUND_COMPLETED`
5. `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.COMPLETED`

<Info>
With **008**, `OUTGOING_PAYMENT.COMPLETED` arrives after the refund settles.
This means the payout was delivered even though the sender was refunded. See
the Manual Cancellation flow in
[Transaction Lifecycle](/platform-overview/core-concepts/transaction-lifecycle).
</Info>
